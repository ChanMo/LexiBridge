# packs/en-ja: data sources

- **Definitions**: EJDict-hand (https://github.com/kujirahand/EJDict, commit 9663055), public domain
  (CC0 1.0). Modified: core senses only, usage notes dropped, shortened (see `tools/build_packs.py`).
  Words it lacks are left out.
- **Phonetics**: ECDICT (https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT, commit bc015ed), MIT License,
  see `LICENSE` in this directory.
- **Levels a2, b1, b2**: CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.6, © Tono Laboratory, Tokyo University of Foreign
  Studies, free for research, education and commercial use with citation. Modified: single-word
  headwords only, each at its lowest level.
- **Level gre**: ECDICT exam tags.

The levels are a reference, not an official list of any exam.

CEFR-J citation:

> The CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.6. Compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies. Retrieved from https://www.cefr-j.org/download.html on 08/10/2026.
