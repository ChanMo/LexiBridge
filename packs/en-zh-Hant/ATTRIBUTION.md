# packs/en-zh-Hant: data sources

- **Definitions, phonetics, exam tags**: ECDICT (https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT, commit bc015ed),
  MIT License, see `LICENSE` in this directory. Converted from Simplified to Traditional Chinese
  at build time with OpenCC `s2tw` (Apache License 2.0) and the phrase list `tools/zh-hant-phrases.tsv`.
- **Levels a1, b1, b2**: CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.6, © Tono Laboratory, Tokyo University of Foreign
  Studies, free for research, education and commercial use with citation. Modified: single-word
  headwords only, each at its lowest level.
- **Levels adv, gre**: ECDICT exam tags.

The levels are a reference, not an official list of any exam.

CEFR-J citation:

> The CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.6. Compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies. Retrieved from https://www.cefr-j.org/download.html on 08/10/2026.
